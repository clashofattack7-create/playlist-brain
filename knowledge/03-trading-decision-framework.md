## 1. The core lens — how this course sees the market

The course does NOT trade classic patterns blindly. Its philosophy (from *Stoploss Hunting's vs Liquidity*, *SL HUNTING Psychology*, *HOW SMART MONEY WORK*):

- **The market hunts stops.** Every move is read as: *whose stops are pending, who is trapped, who must exit.*
- **Classic patterns (breakout, W, M) stop working once everyone uses them** — the course says ~68 chart patterns exist, only 10–12 are used regularly, and stop-loss hunting deliberately breaks them. That's why the course trades **stop-loss hunting & liquidity logic**, not the pattern.
- **"Chart repetition won't happen."** If the same move happened yesterday, the market is engineered to do something else today.
- **Retail is the fuel.** Operator/big-player logic: trap buyers or sellers, take their stops, then move.
- **Every momentum move is one of three things** (*How To Place STOP-LOSS class-12*):
  1. move to **trap buyers**,
  2. move to **flush out sellers**,
  3. **profit booking**.
  The chart must tell you which one it is BEFORE you act.
- **The market is read across 2–3 indexes together** (Nifty + Bank Nifty + Sensex). One index alone lies; comparing indexes exposes the operator's intent.

## 2. The morning checklist (decision inputs)

From *How Operator Work 1*, *Round Number Part-1*, *Previous Day Closing Price*:

1. **Previous-day closing price** of all 3 indexes — the course says **~80% of all market information lives in the previous close**.
2. **Today's gap:** Gap Up / Flat / Gap Down — and the **size of each index's gap**.
3. **Round numbers** near the action (24000, 24300, 24500… for Nifty; Bank Nifty 500/1000-point blocks; Sensex 1000-point blocks).
4. **Important levels / sub-numbers** marked from the previous day's chart (swing highs/lows, candle bodies + wicks, max-body zones).
5. **Who is trapped:** yesterday's buyers or sellers — and where their stops sit.
6. **Expiry day?** — special rules apply (see §5).

## 3. Gap framework (the day's plan selector)

From *Gap Up and Gap Down (Part-1…Class-6)*, *How Operator Work 1*, *Round Number Part-1*:

| Opening | Course rule |
|---|---|
| **Gap Down** | If price recovers within the first 1–2 candles → the gap was only to *change psychology* → **call-side (buy) plan**. |
| **Flat / Gap Up (in uptrend)** | No stops above → market is forced to hunt the **nearby sellers** → **sell-side plan to target trapped buyers' exits… only after seller stops are taken**. |
| **Comparing gaps** | The index with the **smallest gap** is the leader: it has pending stop-losses there and will do the **reversal work**. Trade the buying side of the smallest-gap index — "बिंदास पैसा बनेगा" (comfortable money) until it clears its swing / round number / major important number. |
| **No-retracement gap move** | If yesterday the market moved without retracement, don't wait for retracement today — chart repetition won't come. |
| **Expiry-day gap** | Big rallies on expiry can be *showmanship* — don't chase; check the psychology (see §5). |

## 4. Levels framework — where decisions happen

From *Previous Day Closing Price Class-1…Part-7*, *Round Number Part-1…7*, *How To Mark Important Levels (Sub-number) Part-1…11*, *1 Day Important Number Marking*:

- **Previous Day Close (PDC):** the master level. The course marks it on Nifty + Bank Nifty + Sensex every morning. Above it → buyers' zone; below → sellers' zone. Traps are designed *around the PDC*.
- **Round numbers:** psychological magnets — price hunts pending orders and stops parked at round numbers (24000 / 24300 / 24500…). Levels are drawn at the round number *zone* (with the wick/body tolerance), not a thin line.
- **Important levels (sub-numbers):** mark zones where the maximum candle bodies + wicks matched (supply/demand), zones that held 4–6 candles, and zones price broke but re-entered.
- **1-day important number marking:** each morning, mark the ONE number that matters for today (the number around which the most orders/stops cluster).
- **W & M patterns:** only trade them in the **stop-loss-hunting way** — a W after a 400-point fall is usually just a retracement trap, not a reversal.

## 5. Entry & psychology rules

- **Breakout + retest:** after a breakout, wait for the candle close and retracement, then act on the re-break of the swing (*Stoploss Hunting's vs Liquidity*). Never buy the first poke.
- **Operator sitting below:** after a big fall + strong buy momentum, assume the **operator is long from the bottom and retail got flushed** — trade with the operator (*How To Place STOP-LOSS class-12*).
- **Smart money hides its footprint:** a strong rally that gets fully reversed the next day = smart money doesn't want you to know the trend. Expect either a call-side move or a full sideways day, then a **runaway gap** (buy-side gap) within 1–2 days (*Round Number Part-1*).
- **Expiry day:** don't keep selling into a rising expiry market — better take a small stop-loss and sit; the market drains accounts of stubborn shorts. Read WHY the move came before fading it (*Round Number Part-1*).
- **The 3 momentum questions** (trap buyers? flush sellers? profit-booking?) must be answered before every entry (*class-12*).
- **When to change the current-day plan:** if the opening's psychology is different from the planned scenario (gap changed, level broken differently), REBUILD the plan — the course changes plan instantly instead of hoping (*When To Change Current Day Plan*).
- **Why you fail** (*Why Are You Failing class-17*, *Class 9 CHART HUB*): overtrading, no fixed rules, revenge trades, big positions on weak setups, no stop-loss, and not following the daily plan.

## 6. Stop-loss & money management (the survival math)

From *Money Management Class-15*, *How To Place Stop-Loss class-12/13/14*:

1. **"A small loss is always better than a big regret."**
2. **Capital is the ticket:** you only get the next trade if your capital survives. Protect it first.
3. **Position sizing math (the course formula):**
   - Risk = a fixed % of capital (course example: **1% risk**).
   - ₹1,00,000 capital → ₹1,000 risk → SL distance ₹20 → **quantity = 1000 ÷ 20 = 50**.
   - Bank Nifty lot example: Q = 30, target ₹80, SL ₹60 → max profit 30×80 = ₹2,400, max loss 30×60 = ₹1,800.
   - **Quantity is always derived from RISK, never from greed.**
4. **SL placement:** put the stop where the setup dies (beyond the hunted zone), NOT where you can't bear the loss. The market doesn't care about your emotions, position or dreams (*Class-15*).
5. **Never risk the whole pocket** on one trade.
