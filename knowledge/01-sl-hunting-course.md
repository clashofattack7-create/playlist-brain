## The big idea
The market is an operator-driven game. Prices move to **hunt stop-losses** and take liquidity from trapped buyers/sellers. The course builds a complete decision system: psychology → levels → entries → risk. It explicitly teaches that classic patterns fail once popular, so every setup is read through **who is trapped and where their stops are**.

## Module-by-module knowledge map

### M1 · Market Mechanics & Operators
- **Stoploss Hunting's vs Liquidity** — why SL hunting never stops working: it is based on pending orders & liquidity, not patterns. Pattern evolution: breakout → candle-close → retest systems all got trapped over the years; W/M patterns now often form only where the move is already complete.
- **SL HUNTING Psychology (Class-3)** — the psychological trap: market shows you a move, then takes the stop, then moves. Capital survival > revenge.
- **HOW SMART MONEY WORK (Class-4)** — smart money hides its footprint; big players keep the market sideways or reverse rallies so retail can't read the trend. Next-day "runaway gap" is the footprint leak.
- **How Operator Work 1** — daily live walkthrough: compare the 3 indexes' gaps; the smallest-gap index carries pending stop-losses and leads the reversal; ride buying until the swing/round/important number clears.
- **Operator Entry Points / Retail Entry Points & Their Mistakes** — where operators actually enter (after flushing retail) vs where retail enters (chase, no plan).

### M2 · Psychology & Mindset
- **Dark Psychology (Class-8/10/11)** — the market manufactures emotions: greed at tops, fear at bottoms, hope in losing trades. Your stop is someone's profit.
- **Mindset & Problem (Class-5/7)** — mindset fixes: journaling, small size, accepting losses as business cost.
- **Chapter-4/5/6 series** — mindset behind option trading; trader's mindset behind profit; reasons of failure in stock market.
- **Why Are You Failing (Class-17)** — no rules, no SL, overtrading, revenge trades.

### M3 · Risk & Trade Plan
- **Money Management (Class-15)** — the math: risk = % of capital; quantity = risk ÷ SL distance. Example: ₹1L capital, 1% risk (₹1000), ₹20 SL → quantity 50. "Small loss is always better than big regret." Never risk the whole pocket.
- **How To Place Stop-Loss (Class-12/13/14)** — SL goes where the setup dies (beyond the hunted zone). Before entry, answer: is this momentum (a) trapping buyers, (b) flushing sellers, or (c) profit booking?
- **What is Trading Set-up (Class-16/18)** — a setup = bias + level + trigger + SL + target, written before the market opens.
- **When To Change Current Day Plan (Class-1/2)** — if opening psychology differs from the plan, rebuild instantly; never hope a broken plan into working.
